Document Excerpt

How AI Evaluates Search Relevance

Introduction
When you type a query into a search engine, artificial intelligence works behind the scenes to determine which results are most helpful to you. This document explains the key factors AI uses to evaluate and rank search results.

1. Keyword Matching
The AI looks for exact or related words from your query in the content. Example: If you search “best laptop 2026,” the AI will prioritize content containing these exact words or synonyms.

2. User Intent Classification
The AI determines what you’re actually trying to accomplish — Informational, Navigational, or Transactional.

3. Content Quality Assessment
The AI evaluates whether the content is accurate, well-structured, and trustworthy.

4. Freshness & Recency
Newer content is prioritized for time-sensitive queries.

5. User Engagement Signals
The AI learns from real user behavior, including click-through rates and dwell time.

Why This Works

Uses simple, accessible language · Breaks down complex concepts into clear steps · Includes concrete examples · Maintains a professional, authoritative tone
